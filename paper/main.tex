\begin{abstract}
We present \toolname{}, a verification toolchain built for coding agents.
The agent writes ordinary Python with \verb|#@| specification comments
that CPython ignores, and the toolchain generates verifiable code from
it, today in Dafny, with a Lean~4 backend planned behind the same thin
intermediate representation. The same specs also compile to runtime
contracts hunted by a symbolic executor, so one surface earns concrete
counterexamples before any proof exists and machine-checked proofs
after. Proof material
lives in an additions-only sidecar behind a whitelist validator, so an
agent can help prove but never assume, and verification failures arrive as
a versioned machine-readable taxonomy. On \benchname{}, a curated 14-task
corpus with a deterministic 63-mutant panel, Claude tiers spanning two
model generations place at 11--94\,\% on proof repair and at 63--77\,\%
against a 78\,\% curated ceiling on spec strength, and the two exams
disagree about which tier is strongest. Opus~5 posts the best repair
column measured (17/18, including the task no earlier tier restored) yet
writes weaker specs than Sonnet~4.6. A raw-Dafny baseline arm is designed
to measure what the scaffolding contributes over handing the model the
prover's input directly. Scoring these exams honestly was the hard part. Mutation-scored
specifications are established, but the refuter changes what the score
means. Adjudicating the same panel by witness-carrying execution and by
the prover, we find strictly one-directional disagreement and show that a
bare prover ``Killed'' verdict conflates proof-apparatus breakage and
safety failures with genuine spec refutation. From eleven silent scoring
defects, including one that floored a vacuous spec at 38\,\% rather than
0\,\% and an answer-key retrieval that recurred across vendors, we distill
three rules, led by publishing a trivial-spec floor beside every reported
rate.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Agents are fluent in Python and not in proof languages, and \toolname{}
takes the position that they should not have to be. The agent writes
ordinary Python with \verb|#@| specification comments, and the toolchain
owns the translation to a proof language, generating verifiable Dafny
today with a Lean~4 backend planned behind the same thin intermediate
representation. Everything downstream is built for a model in the loop. Feedback is machine-readable rather than prose, proof
edits are confined to a sidecar the toolchain can police, and the
evaluation is designed to survive the model gaming it.

We make three contributions.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The system} (Section~\ref{sec:apparatus}). \toolname{}
  verifies a fragment of typed Python with specs as \verb|#@| comment
  contracts, compiled both to runtime contracts and to Dafny. Proof
  material is confined to an additions-only sidecar validated by a
  whitelist (ghost declarations with bodies only), so agent edits can
  help prove but never assume. Failures are a versioned machine-readable
  taxonomy. The fragment is a generation target rather than a filter on
  found code. By an optimistic upper-bound survey, 65\,\% of HumanEval and
  67\,\% of MBPP canonical solutions fall inside it, against 3--23\,\% of
  functions in nine typed OSS libraries.
  \item \textbf{\benchname{} and its exams} (Section~\ref{sec:exams}). A
  curated 14-task corpus, verified to full assurance height, drives two
  exams. Proof repair restores a deleted, provably load-bearing lemma
  pack. Spec writing annotates spec-stripped code, scored on the same
  63-mutant panel as the curated reference. Six Claude tiers across two
  model generations are placed, with intervals at trial and task level.
  Repair columns span 11\,\% to Opus~5's 94\,\% (17/18, including 3/3 on
  the task no earlier tier restored), while on spec strength five tiers
  place at 63--77\,\% against a 78\,\% curated ceiling and the
  generations invert, with Opus~5 at 66\,\% under Sonnet~4.6's 75\,\%. A
  raw-Dafny baseline arm hands the model the prover's input directly,
  with no whitelist and raw verifier errors, to isolate what the
  scaffolding contributes (Table~\ref{tab:baseline}).
  \item \textbf{Honest scoring} (Section~\ref{sec:refuters}).
  Mutation-scoring specs is established for contracts under
  KeY~\cite{knuppel2021} and Dafny~\cite{mutdafny}, but the refuter
  changes what the score means. Adjudicating our panel by a witnessed
  refuter and by the prover, agreement is perfect on the cleanly
  refutable class (49/49), disagreement is strictly one-directional (12
  prover-only kills, 0 witnessed-only), and mechanical classification
  shows none of the 12 is a pure spec refutation. We distill three rules
  from eleven measured scoring defects, ten from our own instrument's
  construction, the newest a cross-vendor answer-key retrieval in which
  Grok~4.6 behind the Cursor agent CLI defeated its read-only mode, its
  sandbox flag, and a wildcard tool-deny list.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Design positions.} \toolname{} is a shallow embedding. The
prover never hosts a deep model of Python. A typed fragment is lowered
directly to the target language under a semantics-pinning preamble, and
the lowering is validated against CPython (below) rather than trusted.
Four positions follow.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Single source of truth.} The annotated Python file is the
  only human-maintained artifact. Specs live in the source as comments,
  the generated Dafny model is regenerated on every run and never
  hand-edited, and the one durable proof artifact is the additions-only
  sidecar. There is no second codebase to keep in sync.
  \item \textbf{The source is the deployed artifact.} Translation-based
  verification leaves the verified model in the prover's language, where
  an application cannot import it. Here the verified function is the
  ordinary Python source itself, imported directly with no adapter layer,
  and the annotations cost nothing at runtime because CPython ignores
  comments (runtime contract checking is an opt-in mode).
  \item \textbf{The ecosystem survives, and adoption is brownfield.}
  Annotated modules remain ordinary Python, so test runners, type
  checkers, linters, and virtual environments work unchanged, with no
  parallel prover build infrastructure entering the project. Verification
  is opted into per function, so critical invariants earn proofs while
  surrounding glue code stays unannotated.
  \item \textbf{The backend is a target, not an identity.} Python
  semantics are pinned once in the lowering catalog rather than
  re-derived per prover. The encoder sits behind a thin backend-neutral
  intermediate representation. Dafny is the shipped backend, a Lean~4
  emitter is planned over the same representation, and identical tasks
  would then carry a second prove and fidelity column.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Spec surface.} Specifications are comments beginning
\verb|#@|, so annotated modules remain ordinary Python that CPython runs
unchanged (Figure~\ref{fig:example}). The clause grammar covers an opt-in
\texttt{verified} marker, \texttt{requires}, \texttt{ensures}, loop
\texttt{invariant} and \texttt{decreases}, and a ghost \texttt{proof}
clause that invokes a named sidecar lemma at a program point. Spec
expressions are Python expressions extended with \verb|==>|, \verb|<==>|,
and bounded \texttt{forall}/\texttt{exists} over finite domains, plus
\texttt{result} and \texttt{old()} in postconditions, a deliberate choice
that keeps every spec executable. A \texttt{requires} or \texttt{ensures}
clause therefore has two compiled meanings, a runtime check and a Dafny
obligation, while \texttt{invariant}, \texttt{decreases}, and
\texttt{proof} feed only the prover.

\paragraph{Pipeline.} A front end enforces fragment conformance (an AST
conformance pass plus strict static typing, backed by an encoder that
hard-fails on any construct without a lowering rule), and the encoder emits
Dafny against a versioned preamble that pins Python semantics the SMT
solver would otherwise get wrong, such as floor division, the floor-style
\texttt{\%} (Dafny's is Euclidean), clamped slices, and truthiness. The
pipeline separates concerns into four gate stages, namely conformance,
types, encoding, and proof. The
encoder is the largest trusted component, so it is validated rather than
argued about. A translation-validation harness compiles the Dafny model
back to executable form and differentially tests it against CPython on
property-based inputs. The runtime rail compiles the same specs to
contracts hunted by a symbolic executor, so a spec earns concrete
counterexamples before any proof exists. That pre-proof regime is exactly
where LLM-written specs live, and it is why the scoring question of
Section~\ref{sec:refuters} matters.

\paragraph{Agent interface.} Everything an agent touches is constrained
and machine-readable. Proof material lives in an additions-only sidecar
file beside the source. A whitelist validator admits only bodied ghost
declarations and rejects axioms, bodiless lemmas, \texttt{assume},
attributes, and non-declaration content, so a sidecar can help prove but
cannot introduce assumptions. Verification outcomes are structured
payloads under a versioned failure taxonomy that names the failure kind
(prover obligations such as postcondition, invariant, bounds, and
termination, sidecar resolution errors, and front-end conformance
rejections) with source positions mapped back to Python lines. The embedding API never prints, never exits, and returns expected
failures as values, properties pinned by tests. The repair loop feeds the
taxonomy back to the engine, which may edit only the sidecar.

\paragraph{The fragment is a generation target.} An AST-level survey with
optimistic method matching (an upper bound) accepts 3--23\,\% of
functions across nine typed OSS libraries, but 107 of 164 HumanEval
(65.2\,\%) and 650 of 974 MBPP (66.7\,\%) canonical solutions. Library
plumbing was never the v1 audience. The kind of function LLMs are asked
to write, and that specs naturally describe, is overwhelmingly
fragment-shaped, and code outside the fragment is refused at conformance
rather than scored.

\paragraph{Proof-repair exam.} Delete the sidecar. The engine must restore
verification within four iterations of structured feedback. Admission
requires the pack to be \emph{provably load-bearing}, meaning deleting it
breaks the proof. This is the same criterion AutoVerus applied in
excluding the 26.9\,\% of DafnyBench tasks needing no proof
annotations~\cite{autoverus}. Eight of our fourteen tasks prove from
invariants alone and are excluded, and six qualify. The screen itself must
be able to fail. An early version asserted breakage the encoder already
guaranteed (deleted lemmas still referenced), so it could not fail for any
task. It now strips the proof clauses too and requires a genuine prover
failure, with an explicit inconclusive verdict.

\paragraph{Spec-writing exam.} Strip every specification. The engine
annotates bare code with no verification feedback (grammar-rejected
proposals are retried, at most three per trial), under a freeze validator
enforcing AST-equality of the comment-stripped source. AutoVerus reports
disciplining models into not modifying the implementation as an open
prompting problem~\cite{autoverus}, and we mechanize the control. The
result is scored on the \emph{same} panel as the curated specs.
Anti-gaming is built in, since a vacuous spec reaches full ladder height
and kills 0/63.

\paragraph{Raw-Dafny baseline.} To isolate what the scaffolding
contributes, a baseline arm hands the model exactly what the prover sees,
the encoder's Dafny module with implementation and specifications frozen
by construction, and asks for the proof file free-form. No whitelist, no
taxonomy, and feedback is raw Dafny output. The same verifier judges both
sides, which is what makes the comparison honest (a baseline that
hand-translates the Python could verify a mistranslation). Accepted
baseline answers are audited for assumption smuggling (\texttt{assume},
axiom attributes, bodiless lemmas), and audited-unsound successes are
reported separately rather than credited.

\paragraph{Protocol.} Engines run headless with all tools denied, in an
empty working directory, under a wall clock, with pinned task ordering,
bounded prompts, and the probe discipline of Section~\ref{sec:honesty}.
This is \emph{retrieval}-hardening, not contamination-hardening. With
tools enabled, an agent once located the curated sidecar, which is public,
and returned it verbatim, a retrieval result wearing a proof-completion
costume. The same class recurred, across vendors, against a stack of
nominal tool denials (Section~\ref{sec:honesty}). Memorization of the
\emph{implementations} is assumed and stated. Five of six repair-roster
functions are HumanEval ports, yet scores span 11--94\,\% on identical
tasks, and the roster's hardest task (\texttt{rolling\_max}, HumanEval/9)
is itself maximally memorized yet resisted every tier of the original
grid (0/6 against \efable{}) before falling to Opus~5's loop.
Pack overlap is quantified rather than assumed away. Two pilot runs
independently derived 8- and 5-lemma \texttt{gcd} packs against the
curated 8-lemma pack, sharing only the entry-point lemma the frozen proof
clauses name (earlier-instrument runs, reported as narrative). Recall of
implementations demonstrably does not purchase proof success. Unlike the
never-public Verus proofs behind AutoVerus's leakage
argument~\cite{autoverus}, our curated sidecars \emph{are} public. We
disclose that and deny retrieval mechanically instead. AutoVerus's
authors-familiar-with-the-tasks disclosure is the kin of our
curated-authorship disclosure (Section~\ref{sec:limits}).

\section{Scoring spec strength honestly}
\label{sec:refuters}

\subsection{Two refuters, one panel}

Prover-adjudicated mutation scoring of contracts is prior
art~\cite{knuppel2021,mutdafny}. What is not established is what each
available refuter actually measures. Our leading observation is
structural. \textbf{Prover-judged scoring requires a baseline that
verifies}, because mutation is detected as ``the proof stopped going
through,'' so the unmutated program must prove (all fourteen of ours do).
LLM-written specifications in the pre-proof regime cannot supply that
precondition. \textbf{Witnessed scoring does not need it}, because a kill
is a concrete counterexample against the mutated program's contracts,
whether or not anything proves. Beneath the structural point, the two
verdicts mean different things. A witnessed kill carries an input. It is
sound but incomplete, since it under-credits strength where execution
crashes first. A prover kill carries no witness, and proof brittleness
(the mutant broke the lemma structure, not the spec) inflates it. We
measured the difference by adjudicating the identical panel both ways
(Table~\ref{tab:dual}).

Three findings, in order of importance.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Perfect agreement on the cleanly refutable class.} All 49
  witnessed kills are also prover kills, and no mutant survives both
  refuters. The scored panel has no clean survivors and no spec blind
  spots (equivalent mutants are adjudicated out of the denominator before
  scoring, following IronSpec's relevance filtering~\cite{ironspec}). Two
  further mutants fell outside the encodable fragment and the prover could
  not judge them at all.
  \item \textbf{Disagreement is strictly one-directional and concentrated
  where concrete execution fails first.} We predicted witnessed kills the
  prover would miss. That cell is \emph{empty}. The 12 prover-only kills
  sit exactly on the crash/timeout class, which is close to a corollary of
  refutation-only crediting, and we say so. The empirical content is the
  empty cell and the kind decomposition.
  \item \textbf{A bare pass/fail prover verdict conflates kill classes.}
  We reclassified the 12 prover-only kills \emph{mechanically}. Apply the
  mutant, run the standard pipeline, and take the per-failure obligation
  kinds from the toolchain's versioned failure taxonomy. A mutant with
  kinds in more than one category counts once, as multi-obligation. The
  result is 7 proof-apparatus,\footnote{One of the seven surfaced as an
  unmapped kind. Dafny's ``function precondition could not be proved''
  phrasing fell through the taxonomy and was resolved from the attached
  raw message. The gap is itself an instrument finding, fixed in a
  taxonomy-versioned update.} 1 safety-VC, 1 termination, and 3
  multi-obligation, and \textbf{none is a pure specification refutation}.
  Our own earlier hand-count had labeled four of them ``genuine spec
  refutations''. The mechanical method dissolved that cell, an instance of
  precisely the conflation this section measures. Binary ``Killed''
  verdicts (the scoring in~\cite{mutdafny}) cannot see this structure. The
  witnessed refuter separates crash from refutation natively.
\end{enumerate}

Stated fairly, the refuters are complementary, not ranked. The prover
reached verdicts concrete execution could not (crash-first paths, symbolic
timeout), but its unique reach here is almost entirely apparatus- and
safety-shaped.

\subsection{The honesty protocol}
\label{sec:honesty}

Protocol first, confession second. Each rule is a failure class, the
invariant that excludes it, and the probe that detects its violation.

\textbf{Rule 1 (Floor).} Vacuous specs pass every stage (vacuity has a
long ancestry~\cite{beer,kupferman}), and a crash credited as a kill is
the implicit-oracle class in disguise~\cite{barr}. \emph{Invariant.}
Publish the trivial-spec floor beside every rate. \emph{Probe.} The floor
arm itself, which must score 0.

\textbf{Rule 2 (Denominators).} If a failing answer leaves the
denominator, failing becomes profitable. A spec refuted at the runtime
hunt once contributed $0/0$, vanishing from its own aggregate.
\emph{Invariant.} Subject-independent denominators. Every attempted task
is scored over the \emph{curated} panel, so a failed answer is $0/N$,
never excluded. \emph{Probe.} An intentionally failing arm must lower,
never raise, the aggregate.

\textbf{Rule 3 (Probes).} A harness bug is indistinguishable from a hard
benchmark. \emph{Invariant.} Run false-perfect \emph{and} false-zero
probes before trusting any score. \emph{Probe.} A known-good arm must
score high, and a known-broken one must score 0 for the right reason.

The rules are backed by eleven defect instances measured on this
instrument (Table~\ref{tab:defects}), each caught by the probe class its
row names, and every one produced a plausible number until it was probed.
This is not a private bug diary. The one published instrument whose
scoring we could inspect, MutDafny~\cite{mutdafny}, reports a binary
``Killed'' with no why-classification, the exact conflation the previous
subsection measures. The conflation class caught us as well, when our own
hand-classification of prover-only kills briefly manufactured a ``genuine
spec-refutation'' cell that the mechanical method dissolved.

\paragraph{The eleventh instance, told in full.} It shows these are
field-wide failure classes, not artifacts of one harness. The
out-of-family engine column, Grok~4.6 behind the Cursor agent CLI
(resolved id \texttt{cursor-grok-4.6-high-fast}), scored 0/36 on proof
repair, with all 126 attempts dead before the prover at the encode
stage. Interrogating the zero (Rule 3) found two colliding causes. First, the CLI interleaves tool-narration prose
with its answer, and our reply extraction, pinned to Claude's fencing,
passed the prose straight into the pipeline (``top-level `I'll' is not a
ghost declaration''). Second, beneath it, the disqualifier. The CLI's
machine-wide code-search tool escaped its read-only ask mode, its sandbox
flag, and every tool-deny variant we tried, including the wildcard. The
raw transcript narrates the escape (``Grep can see the files even when
Read cannot'') and then returns the curated \texttt{gcd} sidecar
verbatim. The extraction bug masked the retrieval bug. Fix extraction
first and the column ships retrieval-inflated. Ship it as measured and it
is a false zero. Neither is a measurement. The holding control is an
OS-level read-deny jail around the agent process, probe-verified in both
directions, with the agent remaining functional and its own search
returning denied on the curated trees. Three lessons generalize.
Answer-key retrieval is not one vendor's behavior, ``read-only agent
mode'' is not retrieval-safe, and tool-name deny lists leak.

\paragraph{The remediated column, and why it matters.} Under the jail,
with extraction fixed for both the sidecar and the annotated-source
answer shapes, the same engine produced a valid out-of-family column
(Tables~\ref{tab:repair} and~\ref{tab:spec}). \egrok{} restored 15 of 18
repair trials in each arm (83\,\%) and reached the same 74--77\,\% spec
cluster as the strongest in-family tiers, with all 97 calls carrying
consistent engine-claimed provenance and zero whitelist rejections. The
pre-flight gate re-verified retrieval-blindness before every run. Two
facts make this the answer to the natural ``a Claude-built instrument
measuring Claude'' objection. The out-of-family engine places inside the
same range as the family, not off it, so the instrument is not calibrated
to one vendor. And it restored \emph{every} repair task by majority,
including \texttt{rolling\_max}, the prefix-maximum monotonicity proof no
Claude tier below Opus~5 could reliably construct (Fable~5 0/6). We
confirmed the \texttt{rolling\_max} pack was constructed, not retrieved.
It is not byte-identical to the curated sidecar (different checksum,
shorter proof bodies for the same source-mandated lemma names) and it
verifies independently. Roster difficulty therefore reflects the proof,
not the prover's family.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\toolname{} verifies a fragment, and several planned components (boundary
guard generation among them) are design, not shipped code. The claims
here are about the built pipeline the exams exercise. The corpus is
small, at fourteen tasks with four thin panels. $k{=}3$ is small and
task-clustered, so trial-level intervals are anti-conservative. We report
task-level numbers beside them, and the 11$\to$94 gradient is instrument
validation, never an engine ranking (adjacent intervals overlap at both
levels). The in-family engines are Claude capability tiers, run at
default reasoning effort (recorded as a condition), with resolved model
identities pinned per row for the original grid and by recorded
invocation for the Claude~5 columns. The curated
references were authored with same-family LLM assistance under human
direction and adversarial review, so distance-to-curated is a
within-family measure and roster feasibility was implicitly defined by
what that family can prove. One out-of-family column (\egrok{}) tests
that concern directly and places inside the family's range while
restoring every repair task, so the roster is not calibrated to one
vendor. One out-of-family engine is not a cross-vendor study, and its
provenance is engine-claimed rather than per-call verified (the CLI does
not report a served model), a disclosed weakness relative to the Claude
columns. The measurements themselves are author-blind (fixed mechanical
panel, prover adjudication). Memorization of implementations is assumed, not
ruled out (Section~\ref{sec:exams} controls). Saturation risk is owned.
Opus~5 reaches 17/18 on repair, so this roster's repair tier is close to
solved, but the corpus is an instrument, not a challenge. An all-hard
roster would have no resolution across today's range and could not
distinguish ``cannot'' from ``harness broken'' (Rule 3). Spec strength
still shows range (63--77 against the 78 ceiling, with no engine
matching the curated panel), and the growth path is a two-tier roster,
this
resolution tier plus a frontier tier (prefix-aggregate lemmas, dynamic
programming pending encoder support, nonlinear arithmetic, packs
requiring new helper functions), plus the planned Lean~4 backend's second
prove and fidelity column over identical tasks. The fragment ceiling is a routing
decision, not a coverage claim. Programs outside the encodable fragment
are refused at conformance rather than scored. The venue is non-archival,
and ledgers and pinned invocations ship in the artifact.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Verified Python.} Nagini~\cite{nagini} verifies statically typed
Python by translation to Viper, and its strict-typing front-end discipline
is precedent for our type gate. Strata-Python~\cite{strata}, the Python
front end of AWS's Strata verification platform, translates Python through
the Laurel intermediate verification language to SMT backends, and
\toolname{}'s thin backend-neutral IR keeps such a second emitter
possible. \toolname{} differs from both in compiling one comment-contract
surface to runtime contracts and Dafny alike, in the additions-only
whitelist-validated proof sidecar, and in treating the agent interface and
its scoring as first-class. The runtime rail builds on
CrossHair~\cite{crosshair}, whose symbolic execution over contracts is our
witnessed refuter.

\textbf{Mutation-scored specifications.} Knüppel et
al.~\cite{knuppel2021} score JML contract completeness by mutation under
KeY. MutDafny~\cite{mutdafny} does the same against the Dafny verifier
(40 operators, 118{,}458 mutants, 82\,\% average score) with a binary
Killed verdict, excluding Invalid and TimedOut mutants from the score as
we do. IronSpec~\cite{ironspec} mutates the \emph{specification} and
pioneers automated mutation-relevance filtering, precedent for our
adjudication. Groce et al.~\cite{groce} use mutants to probe what
verification establishes, and mCoq~\cite{mcoq} brings mutation to Coq
proofs. The oracle-quality line (Jahangirova et al.~\cite{jahangirova})
is the test-oracle-shaped ancestor, and Barr et al.~\cite{barr} name the
implicit-oracle (crash-crediting) class our Rule 1 instance exhibits.

\textbf{LLM-written specifications.} The nl2postcond
line~\cite{nl2postcond} scores generated postconditions by bug-catching
power, proof-free. FMCAD'24~\cite{fmcad24} applies symbolic-test metrics
to generated specs for verification-aware languages. Our addition is the
per-mutant \emph{pairing} of witnessed and prover verdicts on one
surface, and the measured strength$\neq$provability divergence.

\textbf{Proof synthesis and benchmarks.} AutoVerus~\cite{autoverus}
generates Verus proofs (137/150 on Verus-Bench, itself translated from
Diffy/MBPP/CloverBench) and grounds three of our protocol positions,
namely benchmark translation as standard practice, the
never-public-proofs leakage argument, and the no-proof-needed exclusion
our load-bearing screen mechanizes. DafnyBench~\cite{dafnybench}
evaluates proof synthesis at scale with whole-error-message feedback.
Clover~\cite{clover} checks consistency among code, specs, and docs, and
dafny-annotator~\cite{dafnyannotator} learns to insert Dafny annotations.
